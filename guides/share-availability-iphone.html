How to share your availability from iPhone

Skip the “when works for you?” thread. Send your open times, or a link people can book from.

Both are part of Tidemark Pro, which you can try free for 7 days.

Send your open times as text

In Tidemark, open Share Availability, choose a length (say 30 minutes) and how far ahead to look, and send the list of open times in Messages or email.

Share a booking link

  1. Choose the length, the days ahead, and any breathing room around your other events.
  2. Share the link. Guests pick a time and, if you like, a place — no app or account needed.
  3. You get the request in Tidemark and tap Accept or Decline; nothing goes on your calendar until you do, and the guest sees your answer.

End-to-end encrypted. Your open times travel inside the link itself, and requests are sealed so no server can read them. There's no scheduling account to set up.
