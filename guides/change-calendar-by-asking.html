Guide

Move or cancel calendar events just by asking

Rescheduling usually means opening the event, finding the date picker and fixing the end time too. In Tidemark you type what you want, in plain English, and confirm.

Try these

  • “Move the dentist to Friday at 3” — a new day and time.
  • “Push my 2 pm back 30 minutes” — by how much, earlier or later.
  • “Move dentist to tomorrow morning” — no time given, so Tidemark picks the first opening that morning that the event fits in.
  • “Cancel lunch tomorrow” — removes it, after you confirm.
  • “Clear my afternoon” — lists what's there so you can cancel one at a time; nothing goes by accident.

You see the change before it happens

Every request shows a card first: the event, its old time crossed out and the new one, plus a warning if it would overlap something else. Tap Move It or Cancel Event to make the change, or Not Now to leave it. After a change, Undo puts it back. For a repeating event, only that one occurrence moves.

Where to find it

On iPhone and iPad, open the Ask tab. On the Mac, press ⌘J. It works with the calendars you already have — iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange and Yahoo. Calendars you can't edit, such as subscribed feeds, are left alone.

Private by design

Tidemark reads your request on your device, with Apple Intelligence where it's available, and the change goes straight to your calendar. There's no account and nothing is sent to a server.
