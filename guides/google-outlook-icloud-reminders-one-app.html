Guide

Every calendar and your reminders, in one app

Work on Outlook, family on iCloud, school on Google, and to-dos in Apple Reminders: Tidemark shows them together, in one timeline, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

1. Add your accounts once

Tidemark uses the accounts already on your device, so there's no separate sign-in. On iPhone and iPad go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts; on the Mac, System Settings › Internet Accounts. Add Google, Outlook, Exchange or Yahoo there, and their calendars appear in Tidemark straight away.

2. Your reminders come along

Reminders from Apple Reminders show up on Today, in the calendar views and on the Reminders page. Tap one to edit its title, notes, date, list or priority, and unfinished ones roll over to the next day.

3. Add any feed

Holidays, sports fixtures, school terms or any webcal:// or .ics link: on iPhone and iPad open Settings › Calendars & Feeds › Add Feed; on the Mac, Accounts & Feeds in the sidebar.

4. Then just ask

Because everything is in one place, you can ask across all of it: “What's due this week?”, “Am I free Friday at 3?”, “Move the dentist to tomorrow morning.” The answers are worked out on your device.
